Privacy Policy

Template text. Have it reviewed for your jurisdiction and your actual deployment before launching publicly.

What we collect

  • Account details: username, optional email, and password (stored hashed).
  • Content you add: notes, uploads, resumes, presentations, quiz results and chat messages.
  • Optional profile details such as your avatar, university and target exam.

How AI features work

When you use an AI feature, the text or file you submit is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a response. Do not submit information you are not allowed to share. Notes you mark as shared are visible to other signed-in users; private notes are visible only to you.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, delete your notes, resumes and presentations, and ask the site operator to delete your account and data.

Security

Passwords are hashed, sessions use secure cookies in production, and uploads are validated. No online service is perfectly secure.

Contact

Use the in-app Send feedback option or the contact address published by the site operator.
